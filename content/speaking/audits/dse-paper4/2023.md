# 2023 HKDSE English Paper 4 Content Audit

Access date: 2026-09-02

## Outcome

The library contains all 24 printed 2023 versions (1.1–8.3) plus one hidden internal mock. The year is **not safe to publish unchanged**: versions 1.1 and 1.3 are assigned to the wrong topics, and twelve versions have a material Part B mismatch or ordering problem. The JSON companion supplies complete replacements while preserving every current stable ID.

The HKEAA sells the authoritative complete paper; only four sample-performance version titles are freely confirmed on the official site. Full-version checking therefore used two scans/OCR copies, then candidate-recall sources as corroboration. Official context passages are not copied. All proposed past-paper contexts are original 150–220-word mock texts.

## Key corrections

- Printed **1.1 is Unmanned Convenience Stores**, not Plant-Based Meat.
- Printed **1.3 is Plant-Based Meats**, not Unmanned Store.
- Material Part B corrections are required in: 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, 3.1, 4.2, 4.3, 5.1, 5.2, 5.3.
- Current generic contexts should be replaced by the topic-specific original mocks in `2023.json`.
- The hidden wearable mock remains a mock and remains hidden; its context is expanded to meet the audit length standard.

## Material conflicts

- **1.1 — Unmanned Convenience Stores**: The canonical 1.1 and 1.3 topics are swapped. The printed 2023 paper identifies 1.1 as unmanned convenience stores and 1.3 as plant-based meats. Stable set_id is retained here as requested, although its slug becomes misleading.
- **1.2 — Cinemas vs Streaming Services**: Topic and Part A are reliable. The canonical Part B contains the right themes but changes the printed order and merges the home/cinema contrast into other wording.
- **1.3 — Plant-Based Meats**: The canonical 1.1 and 1.3 topics are swapped. The printed 2023 paper identifies 1.3 as plant-based meats. Stable set_id is retained here as requested, although its slug becomes misleading.
- **2.1 — Night Owls**: The official HKEAA sample-performance PDF confirms version 2.1 as “Night Owls”. Canonical Part B contains one unsupported replacement question and does not preserve the printed order.
- **2.2 — Teachers and Students on Social Media**: The canonical title is too broad. One Part B question about Instagram is missing, while school safety education is duplicated in adapted form.
- **2.3 — Graduation Celebration**: The scenario and Part A are close, but the canonical Part B is largely reconstructed and does not match the printed question set or order.
- **3.1 — Fashion after the Pandemic**: Part A is sound. Canonical Part B loses the distinction between liking fashion and shopping for clothes, and changes the printed order.
- **4.2 — Adaptability Quotient**: Canonical Part B changes the printed sequence and replaces the question about the importance of new experiences with a different future-oriented question.
- **4.3 — Social Media and Well-being**: The verified article concerns using social media positively. Canonical Part B changes the order of real-life, addiction and self-control questions.
- **5.1 — Family History and DNA Tests**: Canonical Part B reorders most questions and adds an unsupported relationship-maintenance question in place of interest in family history.
- **5.2 — Selling Donations Online**: The verified scenario specifically involves student donations sold through Carousell. Canonical Part B omits whether many Hong Kong people sell online and changes the printed order.
- **5.3 — Online Fitness Classes**: Canonical Part A is sound, but most Part B questions do not preserve the printed wording or order and several are unsupported replacements.

## Integration warning

This audit preserves those IDs and replaces the records they address, which leaves two misleading slugs.
