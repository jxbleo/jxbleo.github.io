# 2019 HKDSE English Paper 4 content audit

- Access date: 2026-09-02
- Past-paper records: 24 (expected 24)
- Hidden internal mocks: 2 (expected 2)
- Copyright rule: official context passages are not reproduced; every proposed PP context is original practice material and is labelled accordingly.

## Main findings

1. The Set sequence is coherent: 1.1–8.3, with three Sets in each of eight groups.
2. The printed candidate-paper scan directly supports the context scenario and Part A wording for Sets 1.1–7.2. The late Sets 7.3–8.3 are supported by candidate-recall indexes; official HKEAA materials independently confirm 8.2 as “McSleepers”.
3. The current Part A points are substantively accurate. Sets 7.3–8.3 remain `close`, not `exact`, because a freely accessible printed page was not located.
4. Only Set 1.1 has a freely accessible official examiner sheet containing all eight Part B questions. Its eight questions should be corrected to the exact official wording included in the JSON. All other Part B lists are useful practice paraphrases but must not be labelled verbatim official questions.
5. The existing PP context paragraphs are generic templates and often do not include the facts candidates need from the printed scenario. They are therefore classified `unsupported` and replaced with 24 original, topic-specific practice contexts of 150–220 words.
6. The two hidden mock Sets are internally coherent and original. They remain separated from the PP library and receive clearer non-official source notes.
