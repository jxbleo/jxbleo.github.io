# 2016 HKDSE English Paper 4 Content Audit

Accessed: 2026-09-02

## Outcome

- Audited all 27 sets against a complete examination scan.
- Confirmed all set numbers and core topics.
- Confirmed the three substantive Part A discussion points for every set.
- Found incorrect Part B ordering in 24 of 27 sets; only 9.1–9.3 were already in printed order.
- Replaced every generic Context with an original 150–220-word, 3–4 paragraph practice text in a natural source genre. These are explicitly not official articles and contain no candidate-facing meta-instructions.
- Preserved every stable `set_id`, `point_id` and `question_id`.

## Important interpretation notes

- `exact` means the field matches the scan or an independently corroborated index.
- `close` means the meaning is correct but wording, punctuation or the editorial title differs.
- `conflict` means the current value changes task meaning or printed order.
- `unsupported` marks the old generic Contexts that were removed. Their replacements are original, topic-specific practice texts rather than claims of official wording.

## Replacement-file contract

`2016.json` contains complete replacement fields for title, source note, Context, Part A and Part B. It preserves all stable IDs. The `order` values on Part B questions reflect the official printed order while each existing `question_id` remains attached to the same question meaning.
