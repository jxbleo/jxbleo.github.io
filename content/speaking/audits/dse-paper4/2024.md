# 2024 HKDSE English Paper 4 content audit

Audit date: 2026-09-03

## Outcome

The canonical library contains 24 past-paper records (versions 1.1–8.3) plus one hidden mock. The 24-version sequence is supported by a full scanned paper, while HKEAA's own 2024 performance materials independently confirm selected versions. The current Part B bank contains substantial conflicts: many items are expanded, reordered or newly invented rather than matching the printed examiner questions. The existing generic contexts are unsupported. Every past-paper record should therefore receive the corrected title/task/question data and an original 150–220-word practice context from the companion JSON.

The hidden mock is not a twenty-fifth official paper. It intentionally reuses the 1.1 topic and must remain hidden and labelled as an independent mock.

## Status key

- **exact**: normalised wording matches.
- **close**: same tested idea with modest wording difference.
- **conflict**: different idea, order or unsupported expansion.
- **unsupported**: no reliable basis for presenting the current field as past-paper content.

It is not appropriate to republish the official context passages. The replacement contexts are original practice articles, clearly labelled non-official.
