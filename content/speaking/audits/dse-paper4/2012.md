# 2012 HKDSE English Paper 4 content audit

Audit date: 2026-09-02

## Result

- Audited all 24 records for 2012. All are visible past-paper practice records; no hidden/mock records were present in the 2012 subset.
- The 24-set structure (Sets 1.1–8.3), topic mapping and Part A discussion prompts are supported by two independent public indexes.
- The HKEAA page confirms that the 2012 English Language examination report and question-paper publication exists, but it does not expose the complete Paper 4 text on that page.
- Set 3.1 has stronger support because a public archive preview reproduces its task and three discussion points.
- Part B questions are supported by one detailed secondary transcription. They are retained as normalized practice wording and classified “close”, not “exact”.
- No official Context passage was verifiable for the complete 24-set collection. Every Context has therefore been replaced with an original 172–188-word mock text and is explicitly labelled as non-official.

## Important limitations

1. “Close” means the educational meaning and set mapping are cross-referenced, but capitalization, punctuation or exact official phrasing may differ.
2. The detailed secondary page paraphrases some prompts. It must not be presented as an official transcript.
3. Context text in the replacement JSON is original practice material. It must retain its non-official label in the product.
4. No set should be deleted or renumbered on the basis of this audit; both independent indexes support 24 tasks in 2012.
