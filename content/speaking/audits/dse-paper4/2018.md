## Status definitions

- **exact**: directly supported by the scan and in the same printed order.
- **close**: semantically faithful normalized or product-facing adaptation, not claimed as verbatim.
- **conflict**: canonical order differs from the printed examination order.
- **unsupported**: no evidence that the canonical prose is the examination source text.
- **original_mock**: newly authored practice prose, explicitly separated from official content.
