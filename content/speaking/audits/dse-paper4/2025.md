# 2025 HKDSE English Paper 4 content audit

- Accessed: 2026-09-03
- Scope: 24 recalled PP records + 1 hidden mock
- Canonical file changed: no
- Context policy: all replacement contexts are original mock practice passages; none is presented as an official HKEAA passage.

## Overall finding

The 24-version topic map is well corroborated. Public sources do not provide a complete official searchable transcript of every task sheet, so exact wording—especially Part B wording and order—must remain labelled as reconstructed. The strongest confirmed canonical defect is Set 6.1, whose first three Part A prompts currently duplicate promotion and omit how to deal with unwanted clothing.

A second source-quality problem affects one external daily-recall page: its 2.1 Shenzhen heading is followed by the 2.3 phobia Part B questions, and its 2.3 heading repeats the 2.2 driving Part B questions. These are page copy-and-paste errors; the audit preserves the correctly grouped travel, driving and phobia question sets.
