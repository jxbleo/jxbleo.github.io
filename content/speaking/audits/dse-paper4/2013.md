# 2013 HKDSE English Paper 4 content audit

- Accessed: 2026-09-02
- Records audited: 30
- Past-paper records: 30
- Hidden/mock records in the 2013 slice: 0
- Outcome: all 30 Set numbers and topic/task families were independently corroborated. The current library is structurally complete, but its contexts are generic adaptations and several titles/task instructions are too broad.

## Material findings

1. **Set 10.1 is a genuine title conflict.** The current title “Promoting a New Society” hides the central Chindogu theme. Replace it with **Chindogu Society** and make the task explicitly about setting up that society.
2. **Part A task situations need restoration.** The current data often says only “preparing a presentation/group task”. The replacements identify the actual product or motion (presentation, letter, brochure, debate, workshop, society, etc.) without claiming verbatim reproduction.
3. **Discussion points and all eight Part B questions are substantially supported.** They are normalized/paraphrased rather than guaranteed verbatim because the available public scan has OCR errors and the other indexes editorialize wording.
4. **Question order is not treated as an identity change.** Some examiner-page transcriptions list Part B in the opposite direction from the current library. Stable question IDs and the current pedagogical order are retained as instructed.
5. **Every Context is replaced by an original mock.** Each replacement is 150–220 words, topic-specific and labelled as non-official. No official article is reproduced.

## Confidence and copyright boundary

The audit is high-confidence for the existence, numbering, topic and semantic content of all 30 Sets because the HKEAA-branded examiner scan, Chenglish task index and at least two year/topic indexes agree. It is not a claim that the normalized English is verbatim official wording. The official HKEAA catalogue confirms the 2013 publication but does not expose the complete paper for direct full-text verification. All replacement Context texts are newly authored practice material and are clearly labelled as mocks.
