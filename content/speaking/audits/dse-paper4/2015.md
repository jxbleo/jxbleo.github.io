# 2015 HKDSE English Paper 4 Content Audit

Audited: 2026-09-02
Scope: all 27 canonical records for exam year 2015

## Outcome

- The year, Set numbering and the three substantive Part A discussion points for all 27 Sets are supported by the examination scan and the independent index.
- The fourth prompt (Anything else you think is important) is also present throughout the examination paper.
- The existing library's Part B question ideas are generally relevant, but Sets 1.1 through 8.3 reorder the official questions. Sets 9.1-9.3 retain the examination order. The JSON replacement restores the examination order for every Set and normalises OCR errors.
- The existing context passages are generic generated prose and are not supported as examination articles. Every replacement context is newly written, specifically tied to that Set, 150-220 words long, and explicitly labelled as a mock adaptation.
- Titles and Part A instructions are concise product adaptations. They remain semantically faithful but are not labelled as verbatim HKEAA wording.

## Status definitions

- **exact**: supported directly and in the same order by the exam scan.
- **close**: semantically faithful adaptation or normalised wording, not claimed as verbatim.
- **conflict**: the existing canonical order differs from the exam paper.
- **unsupported**: no evidence that the existing text is an examination source article.
- **original_mock**: newly authored practice material, clearly separated from official content.

## Import caution

Do not present the mock contexts as official HKEAA passages. Preserve every stable set_id, point_id and question_id when merging. Existing generated reports should retain their content snapshots and must not be rewritten by this library correction.
