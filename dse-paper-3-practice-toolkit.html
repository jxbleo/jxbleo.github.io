第十三章　原创示例与练习

使用方法 先遮住录音稿。由老师／同学以自然速度读两遍：第一遍完成任务，第二遍核实。独自练习时可把文本交给语音工具朗读，但不要边看边听。

Maya: The volunteer briefing was originally scheduled for Friday, 12 September, at 4:30 in Room 402. Unfortunately, the room is being repaired. Let’s move it to Saturday the 13th. 练习 2：观点与语气（Level 5**）

任务：选择最准确的答案，并写出两项证据。

How does Leo feel about moving the exhibition online?

  • A. Completely enthusiastic
  • B. Cautiously supportive
  • C. Strongly opposed
  • D. Uninterested

Evidence 1: ________________________________________________

Evidence 2: ________________________________________________

录音稿（完成后再看）

Leo: An online exhibition would certainly reach people who cannot visit in person, and the interactive map is a clever idea. I’m not fully convinced that photographs can capture the texture of the artwork, though. If we go ahead, I’d keep a small physical display as well and review visitor feedback after the first month.

练习 3：信息分类

把下列笔记放入适当类别：事实 Fact、意见 Opinion、决定 Decision、条件 Condition。

S2: evening sess more welcoming
final: run Tue 7pm
if reg ≥ 20 → add Thu sess

练习 5：快速笔记压缩

把每句压缩成不超过 6 个英文单词／符号。

  • The workshop has been postponed from Monday morning to Wednesday afternoon.
  • The main reason for the falling attendance is the inconvenient location.
  • Participants must bring identification unless they have registered online.
  • Nina supports the proposal, although she is worried about the cost.

第十四章　考场执行清单

录音开始前

  • □ 确认文具、题本和答题本；只在正确位置作答。
  • □ 翻到指定页；利用所有读题时间，不发呆。
  • □ 每组题标出导航词、答案类别、NOT／EXCEPT 等危险词。
  • □ Part B 只做事先决定的 B1 或 B2；不要临场同时阅读两套。

录音进行中

  • □ 当前位置明确；眼睛在当前题与下一题。
  • □ 数字、专名、否定、转折、决定优先记录。
  • □ 遇到改口更新笔记；不要保留两个未区分答案。
  • □ 漏题写 ? 并前进，不让一题拖垮一组。
  • □ 间隙补拼写、单复数和单位。

听力结束后／Part B

  • □ 写出每项任务的 Role / Audience / Purpose / Genre / Content / Tone。
  • □ 把录音和 Data File 资料标到任务点；先完整，再润色。
  • □ 不逐字搬资料；改人称、时态、语气和文体。
  • □ 最后检查任务覆盖、答案位置、拼写、数字、题号和未填空格。
